Compute tight blocker footprints from mesh vertices.

- Replace AABB-based blocker detection with a footprint built from each
  blocker mesh's actual vertex X/Z extents. This removes the false empty
  gaps around houses caused by loose AABBs.
- Only MeshInstance3D blockers are used for footprints (the common case for
  houses, streets, sidewalks, and trees).
